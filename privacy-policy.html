Business Card – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android app “Business Card” (package com.kuncevic.businesscard), made by [YOUR NAME] (“I”), handles information. In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and I do not run a server that receives your data.

Who is responsible

[YOUR NAME] is the developer of the app and the contact point for this policy. Email: [YOUR EMAIL].

What the app handles and where it goes

Entries you add

You can add entries to the CV and the portfolio: a title, a description and, for portfolio entries, a link to a picture. These are saved only on your device, in the app’s private storage. They are not sent to me or to anyone else. The app also remembers which of its built-in sample entries have been deleted.

Language choice

The language you pick in the menu is saved on your device.

Contact form

If you use the “Contact me” form, you type your name, your email address and a question. The app does not send these itself. It opens your email app with a message that is already written and addressed to me. Nothing leaves your phone unless you press Send in your email app. If you do, the message travels through your email provider, and I receive your name, your email address and your question.

Pictures from the internet

Portfolio pictures are not stored in the app. They are downloaded from the web address saved in the entry. The server that hosts a picture receives your IP address and basic technical details, such as the app’s user agent, like any website you visit. Downloaded pictures are kept in a temporary cache on your device so that they load faster. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app removes them.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission: internet access, to download those pictures. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it does not use an advertising ID.

How I use the information

I use messages from the contact form only to read and answer your question. I do not use them for advertising and I do not send you anything unrelated. Data stored on your device is used only to show you your own CV and portfolio.

Who it is shared with

I do not sell your data, and I do not share it with advertisers or analytics companies. The only parties involved are:

  • your email app and email provider, when you choose to send a message;
  • the provider that hosts the pictures (for example a web or code hosting service), which sees your IP address when a picture loads;
  • Google: Google Play and Android may collect technical information, such as crash reports, under Google’s own privacy policy and independently of this app.

I may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Entries and settings stay on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Emails I receive through the contact form are kept only as long as needed to answer you, and then deleted, or sooner if you ask me to.

Security

Your entries are stored in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal (not rooted) device. Pictures are loaded only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or EEA, you can ask me for access to the data I hold about you (for example, a message you sent), ask me to correct or delete it, object to its use, or complain to the data protection authority in your country. Write to [YOUR EMAIL]. The legal basis for answering your message is your request, because you chose to contact me, and my legitimate interest in replying.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If the app changes how it handles data, I will update this page and its date.
